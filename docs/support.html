Support

Detour opens Google Maps links in Apple Maps. If something is not working, the steps below fix almost every case.

Turn the extension on

iPhone and iPad: Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Detour, then turn on Allow Extension. On iOS 17 and earlier the path is Settings › Safari › Extensions.

Mac: Safari › Settings… › Extensions, then tick Detour.

Allow it on Google sites

On the same settings page, under Permissions, set google.com and the other Google sites to Allow. Safari also asks the first time you open a Google page; choose Always Allow. Detour cannot work on a site it has not been allowed on.

Directions still open Google Maps or the App Store

  1. Check the permission above. The Directions button lives on google.com, so google.com must be allowed.
  2. Open the Detour app and press Test it. If Apple Maps opens with directions to Apple Park, the extension is working and the problem is the specific link. Please report it (below) with the link.
  3. Make sure Safari is the browser you are tapping the link in. Safari extensions do not run in other browsers or in in-app browsers inside other apps.

A link opened a map instead of directions, or the wrong place

Please send the Google link. Google has dozens of link formats; the converter is tested against all the known ones and new ones are added quickly when reported.

Turning it off temporarily

Tap the Detour button in Safari's toolbar (on iPhone, the AA or puzzle-piece menu in the address bar) and switch off Redirect to Apple Maps. Links open normally until you switch it back on.

Report a problem

Open an issue at github.com/SteveRyherd/detour/issues. Include the device, the OS version and, if you can, the link that misbehaved. Replace this paragraph with an email address if you prefer email support.

Detour collects no data. See the privacy policy.
