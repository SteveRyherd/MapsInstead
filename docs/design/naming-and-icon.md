# Naming and icon notes

Working notes from the November 2026 rename discussion. The decision is recorded at the bottom
once made; until then this is the shortlist and the reasoning.

## What the name has to do

1. A non-technical person should guess what it does from the name plus the one-line subtitle.
2. It should contain the word "Maps", which is what people type into App Store search, and what
   Apple's own app is called on the home screen.
3. It must not collide with the crowded field. Taken or too close: Mapper, Re-mapper, Map Path,
   Open In Maps, Maps Switcher, MapSwitch, Maps Redirect, Map Redirect, MapLink Router, Rerouter,
   ForceMaps, GoToAppleMaps. That rules out anything built on "redirect", "switch", "router",
   "mapper" or "remap".
4. No "Apple" or "Google" in the name (trademark review risk). Both are fine in the subtitle and
   description as compatibility statements.
5. Short enough not to truncate under the icon on the home screen (about 12 characters).

## Shortlist

| Name | Home screen | Why it works | Why it might not |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Maps Instead** | fits | Plain English. With the subtitle "Google Maps links open in Maps" the whole story is told in six words. Contains "Maps". Nothing like it exists. | "Instead of what?" without the subtitle. |
| Take Me to Maps | truncates ("Take Me to M…") | Warmest, reads like something a person would say. | Too long for the home screen; display name would have to differ from the store name. |
| Links to Maps | fits | Most literal; strong for search ("maps links"). | Flat, and reads like a feature not a product. |
| Detour | fits | Good metaphor (the link takes a detour to Maps), the icon people liked, already the repo name. | Does not say "maps". "Detour" also means delay or inconvenience. Would need a store name like "Detour: Open Links in Maps" to carry the meaning. |

Recommendation: **Maps Instead**, subtitle **Google Maps links open in Maps**. Keep "Detour" as
the internal code name (repository, `DETOUR_*` settings, `DetourMaps` object), which costs nothing.

## Icon concepts

See `icon-concepts.png` (generated by `icon-concepts.py`, PIL only). Six directions, each shown
at 256 px and 48 px:

1. **Detour path (original).** A road that jogs around something and carries on. Distinctive;
   no competitor uses it. Says "route" but not "map" on its own.
2. **Detour path to a pin.** The same road, ending at a map pin. Says both "route" and "map",
   still distinctive, and literally illustrates "the link takes a detour and lands in Maps".
3. **Link becomes a pin.** A chain link, an arrow, a pin. Too fiddly at small sizes.
4. **This pin instead.** A faded pin, an arrow hopping to a solid pin. Fits "Maps Instead" by
   name, but reads as "move the pin", and two pins is Open In Maps' icon.
5. **U-turn.** Clear and bold, but reads as "go back", and orange road-sign colours look like a
   navigation app rather than a utility.
6. **Open in Maps.** A share-style box with a pin rising out of it. Instantly says "open this in
   Maps", though it borrows the iOS share glyph.

Recommendation: **concept 2**. It keeps what people liked about the original, adds the pin so
the subject is obvious, and works at every size. Concept 6 is the runner-up if the name ends up
leaning on "open".

## Decision

Name: **Maps Instead**. Chosen 6 November 2026.

Icon: a split tile, a plain red map pin on a light street map on the left, a white arrow
curving across to a blue navigation arrow on a blue road on the right. Generic pin, generic
navigation arrow, no four-colour Google pin and no Apple compass, so it stays clear of both
trademarks. The source image lives at `docs/design/icon-source.png`; everything in the asset
catalog is cut from it.
